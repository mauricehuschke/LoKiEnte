# LoKiEnte-Logo

Loki-Enten-Maskottchen, erstellt mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug.
Das transparente PNG-Original hat 1254 × 1254 Pixel; es wurde nicht hochskaliert.
Die Vorschau und die ICO-Auflösungen werden mit scripts/Build-Logo.ps1 abgeleitet.
Das Logo enthält keine Schrift, damit es auch als kleines Desktopicon lesbar bleibt.

## Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, funny original mascot logo for the open-source project LoKiEnte: a hybrid of a cheeky duck and Loki, the Norse trickster. One compact centered bust/head mascot, a charming white duck with an orange bill curved into a mischievous grin, one raised eyebrow, a green Norse-inspired helmet with two bold golden curved horns and a tiny green cape collar. Express cleverness and playful coding mischief. Clean professional vector-like illustration, thick dark charcoal contours, very few large shapes, crisp edges, restrained emerald green and warm gold accents, white duck face, orange bill. Readable silhouette at Windows desktop icon size. Original mythological mascot, no actor likeness. No text, letters, watermark, scenery, or accessories beyond the helmet and collar. Square canvas, generous 8 percent safe margins around horns and chin; entire mascot visible. Truly transparent background. Highest available resolution, ideally 2048 by 2048 pixels, suitable as a reusable high-resolution PNG master and Windows ICO source.
